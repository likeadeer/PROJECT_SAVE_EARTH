This project is exploring outer space to save Earth.  
The lore/backstory: The Alien Princess is sent to gather ingredients to throw into a portal, which then makes the Magical Ozone Plant. Once planted, it produces an indistructable ozone layer around the Earth, that was once dull but now reborn. She achieves these ingredients by either solving personal problems, or solving silly riddles. Her origin story: her parent is human, and the other is a purple alien. Humans couldn't survive on Earth anymore, but luckily had good relations with these purple aliens. The aliens took humans in on habitable spaceship, designed to home humans and let the aliens survive for a prolonged amount on it too. They all spent years researching how to revive Earth, and a component of that, is the Magical Ozone Plant. The Alien Princess is one of the few set out on a grand mission, with her own little mission - which is keeping relations with other planets at the same time. In the end, they save Earth, one mission at a time!  
Themes explored: Climate Change (what happened to the Earth), Social Sciences (how relations between two different species developed), Space, and Personal Reflection (the choices you have to make).
Practical: Originally, I intended to make the art digital, but with the lack of money I had, and lack of interest into free sources, I decided to use a sketchpad, pencils, and acrylic paints. I made a whole  plan. In this plan I wanted to paint: 10 ingredients, the princess (us), crystal alien + crystal planet, magical plant, mysterious alien + mysterious planet, plant aliens + plant planet, spaceship, volcanic aliens + volcanic planet, dull dead Earth + reborn Earth, the pink and purple portal that you could drag the ingredients into. I managed to paint it all except the portal, the ingredients and the versions of Earth. I used acrylic as it's the easiest paint to work with, and the fastest. And I don't really know how to explain design choices, there wasn't much inspiration in the moment in terms of how the aliens will look or their functionality, I just thought they were cool and had fun.  
I took photos of the paintings on my phone and then cut them out by creating a sticker, and sending that sticker to my email, then saving that to my laptop as a PNG, and then coded them in from there (I didn't have access to photoshop and thought this was the easiest way, and it worked good enough! I'm satisified). I hope you read the my whiteboard page above, as that shows all my research and a little bit of my progress.
